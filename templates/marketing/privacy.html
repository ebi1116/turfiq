This Privacy Policy explains how TurfIQ collects, uses, stores and protects information when turf owners use our business analytics services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account and business information, customer records entered by account owners, operational activity, income and expense data, support communications, and technical information such as browser, device and usage data.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide dashboards and reports, calculate business metrics, maintain accounts, offer customer support, prevent misuse, meet legal obligations and improve TurfIQ.

3. Subscriptions and payments

Subscription payments may be processed by Razorpay. TurfIQ does not store complete card, bank account or UPI credentials. Payment providers process this information under their own privacy and security practices.

4. Sharing

We may share necessary information with trusted providers that support payments, communications, hosting, security and analytics. We do not sell personal information or share one owner's business data with another owner.

5. Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Information is retained only as long as needed for service delivery, legitimate business requirements and applicable law.

6. Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of eligible personal information by contacting us. Certain records may be retained where legally or operationally required.

7. Updates

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. Material updates will be reflected on this page.
