This policy explains refund and cancellation terms for TurfIQ subscriptions.

1. Free trial

The 30-day Premium trial is free and requires no activation payment. Paid access costs ₹199 per month after the trial.

2. Subscription cancellation

You may cancel future renewal according to the controls and notice period shown in your billing settings. Access continues for any period already paid unless stated otherwise.

3. Refund eligibility

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable after a paid billing period begins, except where required by law or where a verified billing error occurred.

4. Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing generally takes 5–10 business days after approval, depending on the bank or payment provider.

5. Duplicate or incorrect charges

Contact support with your account email and payment reference. Verified duplicate or incorrect charges will be corrected through the original payment method.

6. How to request help

Email support@turfiq.in with your registered email, payment reference and reason for the request.
