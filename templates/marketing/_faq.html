TurfIQ is a business analytics platform for turf owners. It brings revenue, expenses, customer activity and operational trends into one dashboard.

You can track revenue, growth, occupancy, expenses, profit, payment mix, customer retention, sport trends and peak hours.

Yes. TurfIQ supports business report exports in Excel, CSV and PDF formats for the period you select.

Your workspace is account-protected, and each owner can access only the business data associated with their account.
